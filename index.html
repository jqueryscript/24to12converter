How to Convert 24-Hour Time to 12-Hour Time

Keep the minutes the same. Use the hour to decide what to write before the colon and whether to add AM or PM.

00:00–00:59
Replace 00 with 12 and add AM. 00:30 becomes 12:30 AM.
01:00–11:59
Keep the hour, drop any leading zero, and add AM. 09:15 becomes 9:15 AM.
12:00–12:59
Keep the hour at 12 and add PM. 12:30 becomes 12:30 PM.
13:00–23:59
Subtract 12 from the hour and add PM. 21:15 becomes 9:15 PM.

Military Time to Standard Time

The first two digits are the hour and the last two are the minutes. Type 1830 directly into the converter, or read it as 18:30. Both give 6:30 PM.

Convert AM/PM to 24-Hour Time

Select Swap and enter a time such as 6:30 PM. To convert by hand, use 00 for 12 AM and 12 for 12 PM. Keep all other AM hours and add 12 to all other PM hours. Use two digits for the hour and keep the minutes the same.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does 24:00 mean?

24:00 can mark midnight at the end of a day. It means the same instant as 00:00 at the start of the next day. The converter accepts 00:00 through 23:59. Use 00:00 for midnight.

What do AM and PM stand for?

AM stands for ante meridiem, Latin for “before noon.” PM stands for post meridiem, Latin for “after noon.” In 12-hour time, 12:00 AM is midnight and 12:00 PM is noon.
